Correct the evidence; qualify timing separately

Release decision: deploy clearer maker/taker provenance and exact entry-book persistence. The earlier maker-fill option is implemented and tested, but remains disabled. Its profit condition has not been established. Paper mode, $150 base margin, risk caps, evidence rules and current allocations are unchanged.

What was wrong

The last-24-hour paper audit found five maker entries all labeled quote, although the fill was inferred from a limit crossing. All five had a valid post-placement trade-through before expiry, but the median booked delay from that print was 16.257 seconds; SOL #14867 lagged 124.647 seconds. A maker quote at placement does not prove an exchange queue fill.

Thirteen taker entries used depth for pricing, but the durable entry audit retained a separate REST sample taken after entry (median 125 ms later), not the exact book consumed by the pricing pass. That later sample cannot reproduce the original execution decision.

What is released

  • Maker entries now say limit-candle-model or limit-tape-model, with role, placement, expiry, event time, received observation time, and the triggering candle or print. No historical labels or times are rewritten.
  • Taker entries preserve both sides of the exact pricing book, receipt/exchange/evaluation timestamps, pricing quantity and raw VWAP. The fill quantity, reference price and actual booked price remain separate: the existing conservative reference-price floor is preserved.
  • Final admission depth is captured for maker and taker entries, including the checked quantity, contract value and shadow account-equivalent quantity. This is separate from the pricing book and the asynchronous post-entry diagnostic.
  • Evidence writes share the entry transaction. A failed write rolls back the entry and order; a fresh observation can retry. Journal details expose the saved books and maker/taker role, and allocation audit labels the later diagnostic explicitly.

Why earlier maker timing stays off

The optional paper.makerTapeCells rule requires a positive-volume, fresh trade after placement/recovery and before expiry, uses receipt time instead of backdating, rejects candle inference, and retains all existing quote, cost, depth and risk checks. Changed cells get a new strategy identity; candle-only backtests refuse to qualify it. No cells are opted in by this release.

ETH #15466 first traded through 2072.8 at 15:45:43.886 UTC and that print was received at 15:45:44.054. The current candle model booked 15:46:00.143: a 16.089-second avoidable observation delay in the tape scenario. However, the retained ETH book starts with a usable snapshot only at 15:48:34.103. Substituting that later book would use future information. The eventual recorded trade closed profitably, but that does not prove the earlier entry would pass admission or preserve its result.

Required evidence before enabling: continuous trade/depth coverage from placement through exit, causal receipt-time admission at the first fresh crossing, the same funded quantity and exit engine, fees and funding, unresolved trades shown explicitly, and a later evaluation window with no deterioration in net profit. A trade-through remains a paper maker-fill model; actual maker/taker classification comes from exchange execution reports, and visible market data does not establish our queue position.
