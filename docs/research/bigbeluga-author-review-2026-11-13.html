What needs correcting

1. Separate author setups from tested bot interpretations

Kinetic Bull Spike, Asia-only Session Killzones and Pivot Channel long-only are deliberate readings (decisions 67/195). Native alternatives need distinct versions; do not overwrite them or count both as independent evidence.

Kinetic Momentum Vectors · Session Killzones & Breakouts · Pivot Channel Breaks

2. Bind signal names to source conditions

High Volume Pivot S/R Zones distinguishes resistance breakout (long), support breakdown (short), and retests of flipped levels. Generic support/resistance direction guesses are unsafe. Direction-shortening such as Dn needs a local contract, never a global activation.

High Volume Pivot Support & Resistance Zones · Dynamic Trend Bands & Anchored VWAP Signals · Triangular Hull Moving Average + Volatility · Logarithmic Regression Channel-Trend

3. Separate level updates, entries, and exits

Step Range needs state to determine the side of a shared trailing-start marker. Target Trend draws protection and targets as graphics. A correct entry alone cannot reproduce either author lifecycle.

Step Range Breakout & Trailing Stop · Target Trend

4. Use availability time rather than drawing location

Delayed pivots, retrospective patterns, and previous-bar labels cannot create earlier trades. Last-bar redrawing and viewport-dependent tools are research context unless a causal contract is proved.

Supertrend Error-Adaptive Cloud · Visible Range Support and Resistance · Swing Traces

5. Keep candle proxies distinct from measured exchange data

Long/Short Ratio uses candle-derived participation; HTF Delta Flux liquidations are synthetic estimates. Depth, whale, and liquidity visualizations must not masquerade as Delta account ratios, book depth, or exchange liquidations.

Long/Short Ratio · HTF Delta Flux + Liquidations · Levels Strength Index

6. Track behavioural updates and runtime availability

Volume Delta Pivot Matrix changed its delta allocation; the current upstream import is incompatible. Paid Market Core, Market Waves, Nautilus and Strategy Builder have substantive releases, but their public explanations do not expose an executable source contract.

Volume Delta Pivot Matrix · Market Core · Market Waves · Nautilus Oscillator · Strategy Builder v1.0.0

7. Exclude explicitly unsuitable candidates

The author advises against the old MA10/20 script. Manual anchor/visible-range tools and external on-chain tools cannot become autonomous Delta entries merely because a chart displays directional labels.
