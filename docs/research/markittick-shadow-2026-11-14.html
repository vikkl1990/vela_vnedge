What is compared

Both arms share one source run, the same native initial stop, entry logic, reference purse, sizing, full commissions/GST and causal execution. The control uses the account R ladder (currently allocated to 6R), trail from 0.5R and existing profit floor. The candidate uses MESA's 2R target, Squeeze's band target or GARCH's 1.5R target, with no inherited account trail. GARCH models volume-return variability.

These are independent experiments, not a combined $1,000 portfolio. Risk caps and account capital remain unchanged. The $150 current margin is retained to isolate exit behavior; the installer does not impose it as a future mandatory allocation.

Why these pairs

Screened the 30 highest-turnover available complete crypto histories. Ranked by liquidity, not P&L. Required at least two historical signals passing existing regime, fee and sizing rules and all three fresh depth checks at at least $750. Higher-turnover AKE, SOPH and TAC failed the 0.25% book-cost ceiling; no exemptions were added.

Evaluation before paper

Track scan receipts, accepted/rejected signals and reasons, pending orders, actual causal fills, commissions, stop exits, native target exits, net profit, drawdown and hold time. Compare full cohorts alongside matched closed source events; include unfinished and missed trades. A matched subset alone cannot establish improvement because different holding times change future capacity.

These research trades are excluded from promotion, copying and sizing evidence. A promising policy needs its own ordinary prospective shadow record and unchanged shared evidence checks before paper admission. No scanner is paper-enabled here, and no backdated market fill is used as a substitute for live observation.

Validation

Unit and integration tests cover identical causal entry fills, source deduplication, risk and fee preservation, distinct exits, restart durability, atomic/idempotent activation and zero promotion-eligible experiment rows. Public-source lifecycle validation also checks closed-bar prefix stability and synthetic tape/depth fills; those synthetic fills are mechanics tests, not profit evidence.

Source data: Delta India products, tickers, timestamped order books. Full feasibility record.
