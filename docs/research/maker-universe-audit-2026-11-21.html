Limits of this evidence

  • Universe is the current bot scheduled/observed markets, not all markets Delta could ever list.
  • Asset classes use the repository fallback taxonomy; live Delta product tags were not imported.
  • Context sample coverage is not uninterrupted executable tick/depth coverage.
  • Shadow virtual dollar P&L is intentionally not aggregated or compared with account dollars.
  • Replay is conditional on accepted orders; it does not establish portfolio opportunity cost for rejected/expired orders.
  • All entry arms use identical recorded exit opportunities; stream-source switching cannot be claimed as an entry profit gain.
  • Public data cannot prove exchange queue priority. Missing coverage is unknown, never a zero-loss or unfilled win.
