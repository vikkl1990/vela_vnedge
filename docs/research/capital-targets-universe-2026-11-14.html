TP evidence: smaller everywhere did not work

1,044 closed account trades were retrieved; 1,018 had usable paths. Six had no adequate path and 20 had gaps. Tested 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2 and 3R, taking either all or half. A trigger uses the next recorded price with 5 bps adverse exit stress, full gross fees on both baseline and candidate, and no reused capacity. The final 14 entry days were held out.

The only earlier-period positive selection was essentially flat and lost more on later trades. Therefore no global smaller-TP override is enabled. The replay is approximate: minute observations miss intraminute touches, historical policies differ, and earlier exits do not generate hypothetical new entries.

Target policy

  1. Use validated author brackets when the scanner provides executable levels. Observe the actual entry time; reject invalid geometry.
  2. For scripts with context but no trade lifecycle, keep a separately versioned bot exit policy. A chart annotation is not an order.
  3. Compare full native TP, partial TP plus runner, and current trailing policy using identical entries and corrected execution costs. Freeze the selection before later-date evaluation.
  4. Choose by net profit, drawdown, loss frequency and profit surrendered—not target hit rate alone. A version must pass prospective evidence before paper admission.
  5. Keep the displayed target, allocated exit quantity and effective trade policy together in the UI. A distant final runner target is not the expected profit on every trade.
