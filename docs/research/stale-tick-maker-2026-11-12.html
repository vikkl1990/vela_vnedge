What the current bot accepted

30,877 recorded trades; median arrival age 306 ms, 99th percentile 1,002 ms. A single delayed burst contained 74 trades older than five seconds (maximum 13,472 ms). Of those, 55 also passed the engine's ordering check. No exit request or stop acknowledgement was recorded at their arrival timestamps.

The new guard rejects stale, unknown, invalid or excessively future-dated trades before they can refresh marks, claim tape liveness, fill entries or change stops. Raw observations remain available to research; rejected events get a reason. A quiet market's next timely trade is still accepted. The existing stricter one-second maker timing check is unchanged.

Limits of the evidence

  • This is not a full portfolio or out-of-sample profit backtest.
  • The delayed-burst replay seeds states from prior journal samples; exact historical trail transient state and partial candle arrival sequence were not retained. Four unbound held positions are excluded from it.
  • The 638-trade compatibility regression uses fixed recorded quotes and exit inputs; unchanged P&L there is compatibility evidence, not a profitability estimate.
  • The maker comparison measures observed entry support at the same price and deployable size. It does not simulate queue position, counterfactual exits, later portfolio eligibility or independent shadow promotion.
  • Only one of the 25 maker trades was closed at the frozen cutoff. No maker timing profit claim or deployment is justified.
  • Additional paper_bar and paper_closed_minute records start prospectively; no historical observations are invented.
