Shared stop recovery and reserved scalp capacity

Final scope clarified by the operator on 9 November 2026 IST. Only the fixed scalp/swing quotas are withdrawn. Pilots use the shared pool within the existing total capacity and daily ceiling. The 60-minute cooldown remains pair-wide across all scanners and directions. The re-entry results below apply to the retained rule; the capacity-split results describe a proposal that is not enabled.

Two coordination corrections, within the existing budget. A stop now starts the existing 60-minute cooldown for the pair across scanners, directions and timeframes. Paper copy pilots reserve one slot for scalp entries and one for swing entries, inside the existing two-slot total.

Implemented behavior

  • Pair re-entry: checked at entry and deferred market/limit fill. Latest completed stop wins; future timestamps cannot block an earlier event. State is recovered from the account's recorded closes after restart. Other pairs remain eligible. No new stop or target formula.
  • Pilot horizons: entry timeframe below 1h is scalp; 1h and above is swing. Daily reservations: 8 scalp + 2 swing, within 10 total. Open and pending orders both reserve slots. Daily allowance is retained after cancellation and restart, and resets at UTC midnight.
  • Capital: $150 base margin, pilot fraction 1/3, total pilot slots 2, total daily reservations 10 and existing account risk/fee gates retained. Native allocations consume no pilot allowance. Unknown timeframes are refused.
  • Migration: existing trades retain their exits; no forced closure. Two grandfathered swings can temporarily occupy both global slots; a scalp slot becomes available when one exits. Legacy daily reservations with no timeframe classification count conservatively toward both buckets until UTC midnight. Settings exposes all four quotas and explains the shared cooldown.
