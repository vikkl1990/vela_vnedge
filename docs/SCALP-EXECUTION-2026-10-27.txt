Scalping execution correction, 27 October 2026

Problem: a planned resting maker limit was admitted at the executable market quote before its actual price was calculated. Stop levels, fee viability, size, depth and exposure therefore described a different order. A signal reference already across the book could also be recorded as a maker order.

Correction: retain fresh-quote validation, calculate the passive tick-aligned intended limit first, then run all admission calculations at that price. Long limits round down, short limits round up. A crossing signal reference is moved behind the current opposite quote. Fill-time checks remain mandatory. The backtest uses the same directional rounding; historical candles cannot reconstruct contemporaneous quotes or queue position. Existing positions, configured timeframe enrollment, fees, caps and alignment rules are unchanged.

Scalp research protocol: BTC/ETH/SOL, 15m confirmed events from Trend Impulse Channels, Smart Money Breakout Signals and Structure-Anchored VWAP. Compare market entry, 0.1 and 0.25 ATR maker pullbacks expiring after one signal bar, and 0.1 ATR maker with 1/1.5/2R partial exits (50/25/25). Use cached 56-day exchange histories ending 25 October, 28/28 chronological split, current source and reading rules, account-compatible ATR stops, existing regime and 6x cost policy, full modeled commissions, 0.02/0.08 percent round-trip crossing assumptions and extra 3bp stress. These scenarios are independent strategies, not a capital-constrained portfolio. Previously studied history is not pristine out-of-sample evidence. Historical depth, exact funding and maker queue position are unavailable.

Next algorithm to implement and validate, rather than relaxing a global gate:
1. Keep the scanner's confirmed 15m setup as a time-limited directional opportunity, not an immediate entry.
2. Observe completed 1m/3m bars after that setup. Require a pullback and then a reclaim/rejection trigger; use only information available when the trigger closes. Expire an opportunity after its defined setup window. Do not synthesize historical account fills.
3. Place a passive order at the executable trigger price with a short TTL and explicit cancellation when setup structure invalidates. No automatic market chase on expiry. Size from that trigger's structural stop and modeled risk cap.
4. Choose a reachable target from local structure and observed volatility, then evaluate expected net payoff including entry, exit and likely funding. A high stop-distance/fee ratio alone does not establish expected profit. Never widen a stop just to pass it.
5. Test the exit policy as a whole: partial targets, trail activation and a time-based no-progress exit. A tighter target has little effect if the existing trail exits before it. Keep swing exits separate.
6. Compare paired opportunities, fills, missed winners, adverse excursion, net R, drawdown, cost, holding time and throughput, chronologically and under extra crossing costs. Forward-paper validation must precede broad account enrollment.

This commit repairs the existing execution path. It does not claim that a new scalp strategy is profitable or activate unvalidated 15m maker enrollment.
