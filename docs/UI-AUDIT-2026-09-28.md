# Dashboard audit — what the UI must show and do before real money — 28 September 2026

Thirteen pages, three roles, a live SSE feed, a risk banner, trade forensics, per-scanner overlays,
an incubator page, analytics. Judged as an operator's console for a bot that will hold real
positions on Delta India, against what a market-grade trading console gives its operator.

Grades: **P0** blocks going live · **P1** needed in the first live week · **P2** market-grade polish.

## P0 — still blocking

1. **Wallet, margin and leverage are not shown.** Every equity figure on every page is the paper
   book's. Live, the header must show the exchange wallet balance, margin in use and available,
   and each position's exchange-side leverage; today none of that is fetched (`transport.wallet()`
   exists, nothing reads it into the UI). Blocks: the operator cannot see a margin call coming.
2. **Fills are not marked by source.** A fill restated from the exchange carries
   `price_source = 'exchange'` in the orders table; the Trades page does not show it, so a paper
   price and an exchange price look identical. Live, the difference is the slippage bill.
3. **Alerting has no status.** No page says whether Telegram is configured, when the last alert
   went out, or that a halt fired silently. Add a notifications tile: configured / last sent / last
   error, and an alert history (the SSE feed is live-only; reload and it is gone).
4. **Config changes have no audit trail and no guard.** `PUT /api/config` applies at once for any
   trader; nothing records who changed what. SYSTEM.md says the configuration is frozen until the
   live review; the UI does not know that. Needed: an actions log (user, action, before → after,
   time) covering config edits, closes, pauses and resets, and admin-only for exit and risk rules.
5. **The Exchange page is untested against a real account.** It renders the executor's status
   truthfully in paper mode (everything empty). The first demo-host or live session must be spent
   watching it.

## P1 — first live week

6. **Data freshness per symbol.** The header shows one feed dot and a tick age. Live, a stale
   symbol is a stale stop; show last-bar and last-tick age per open position, and the scan lag
   (bar close → signal) distribution the stale-rejection rate hides.
7. **Signals page has no rejection summary.** 65% of signals were being refused for capacity and
   the only place that was visible was a CLI. Add the outcome breakdown the state report has
   (opened / stale / margin / fee filter / halted) as a header on the page.
8. **Incubator page does not show cohorts.** The gate judges scanner × timeframe pools with a
   time-to-verdict; the page still lists pairs. (Open item 1 in SYSTEM.md since decision 29.)
9. **Per-trade cost breakdown.** Fees are a column; funding, slippage against the quote, and the
   Scalper-Offer waiver are in the record and not on the page.
10. **Sessions and roles.** Login has lockout and expiry; there is no session list, no
    "sign out everywhere", no 2FA. Acceptable for one operator behind nginx; not for a shared console.
11. **Mobile.** Fifteen breakpoints and a compact mode exist; the positions table still needs
    ~1,500px for every column. A phone view of "what is open, what is locked, pause" is the one
    that matters at 3 a.m.

## P2 — market-grade

12. Risk dashboard: exposure by side, by market, correlation between open positions (the risk
    layer computes `corrBars`; nothing shows it).
13. PnL calendar and drawdown chart (the equity curve exists; drawdown and daily buckets do not).
14. CSV export of trades and orders.
15. Trade notes and tags for the journal; the manual-close reason is free text nobody reads back.
16. Strategy/config versioning: which config each trade was opened under (finding 12 of the
    original audit, still open).
17. Backtest from the UI with the halves table, not just the single-run result.

## What is already at market grade

Trade-path forensics with the stop history; per-scanner overlays on the chart; the incubator's
shadow book; hotkeys; UTC everywhere with local-time tooltips; error boundaries and skeletons on
every page; SSE with reconnect state; confirmations on every destructive control.
