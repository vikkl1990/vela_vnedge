AINUSD / Trend Impulse 15m confirmed 1h alignment, 27 October 2026

Authorized after reviewing repeated AIN shorts. One explicit rule is added
under paper.entryAlignments for trend-impulse-channels-zeiierman, AINUSD,
15m; context 1h, EMA21. Long requires the latest closed 1h close above a
rising EMA; short requires below a falling EMA. Flat, missing, stale or
gapped context refuses entry. Forming/future context bars are excluded.
No per-day two-stop rule is enabled. Other scanner/market/timeframe tuples,
including the source's ETH/DOGE allocations, do not match this rule.

The same pure rule gates account and shadow entry intent and deferred
market/limit fills, including orders pending across restart. A refused
reversal keeps the held position. Existing position exits are not gated.
Context series are explicitly included in scanner candle dependencies.
Account/Shadow refusal reporting classifies alignment as a regime refusal.
Missing context is fail-closed, so a data outage cannot admit an entry.

runBacktest applies the rule on signal and fill, using explicit context
candles when provided or only complete higher-frame buckets otherwise.
Entry decision time is no earlier than the signal close. A reversal asks
before closing the position it replaces. Partial aggregation buckets are
excluded and bars not yet closed at decision time cannot supply direction.
