# Fees and ATR — how the bot computes them, checked against the exchange and the record — 28 Sep 2026

## Fees

**Model** (`paper/logic.ts: feeFor`): `notional × rate × 1.18`. Taker 0.05%, maker 0.02%, 18% GST on
the fee → **0.059% per taker side, 0.118% round trip**. Take-profit limit legs are charged maker.
Funding is charged per 8-hour slot at the exchange's rate on the open notional at mark. Fills cross
the quoted spread when a quote exists (buy at the ask, sell at the bid) plus 2 bps; without a quote,
2 bps against the reference price. The Scalper Offer waives the closing fee when a futures position
closes within 30 minutes on BTCUSD/ETHUSD and 15 minutes elsewhere; partial closes count;
liquidations and PAXG/SLVON/XAUT are excluded.

**Against the exchange.** Delta India's schedule is maker 0.02%, taker 0.05%, "18% GST is applicable
on trading fees", worked example `$20 × 1.18 = $23.6` — the model to the cent. The Scalper Offer
support article: "0% closing fee on Futures trades that you open and close within the scalping
window", BTCUSD & ETHUSD 30 minutes, other futures 15, "applies to fully and partially closed
trades", not PAXGUSD/SLVONUSD/XAUTUSD, liquidations not eligible — the model term for term. **One
live-trading caveat:** the offer needs a one-time "Join Now" on the account; trades placed before
consent are not eligible. Do that before the first live order or every quick exit pays 0.059%.

## ATR

**Model** (`data/indicators.ts: atrSeries`): Wilder's ATR, length 14 — first value the simple mean of
14 true ranges, then `(atr × 13 + tr) / 14` — on the scanner's own timeframe, closed bars only. The
signal bar is closed when the signal fires, so it is included; there is no unclosed bar in the
series. Live, the scanner feeds its full history window (hundreds of bars), so the recursion is
converged. The fallback stop is `fill ± fallbackAtrSl × ATR` (1.0, decision 42; 4.0 for
structure-anchored-vwap), anchored at the **actual fill**, not the signal's reference price, so the
stop distance is exactly one ATR regardless of how the entry filled. Backtests use `atr[i]` at the
entry bar — the same definition, no look-ahead.

**Against the record.** Trade #485 NEARUSD, signal on the 18:15 bar, filled 18:30:03 at the ask
5.344 (reference 5.348): recomputed Wilder ATR(14) over 449 closed bars ending at 18:15 = **0.0578**;
the bot's stop 5.2862 = 5.344 − 0.0578. Identical to four decimals. (A simple 14-bar mean of true
range would have given 0.0589; the two differ by ~2% here and can differ by 20% after a spike —
Wilder is the one TradingView's `ta.atr` uses, so scripts and stop agree.)

## What follows

1. Nothing in the fee or ATR arithmetic needs changing. Both match the exchange and the record.
2. **Majors on 15m are structurally uneconomic at these fees.** Either move the BTC/ETH/BNB pairs to
   1h (their 1h ATR is 0.5–0.7%, clearing the filter) after a survey shows the scanners hold at 1h, or
   take them out of the 15m fleet. Leaving them costs nothing in money and something in scan time
   and in attention on the Signals page.
3. Show fee-in-R next to each rejection and on the scanner page, so "stop too tight" reads as
   what it is: this market's volatility cannot pay a 0.118% round trip at this timeframe.
4. Before the first live order: opt in to the Scalper Offer on the account.
