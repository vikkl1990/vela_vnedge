How this was built

  1. Each trade keeps its real fill prices, stop, exit and times. Profit and fees scale with the position, so the Scalper Offer's waived closing fee carries over.
  2. The record uses only shadow trades that closed before the trade opened, so nothing from the future sets the leverage.
  3. Leverage is capped where it would put liquidation within 1.25 times the stop distance, using each market's maintenance margin on Delta India.
  4. "Stop would cost" is the loss had the trade hit its original stop, fees included.
  5. A signal is skipped when 5 trades are already open, counting the account's positions that are still open now.
  6. Trades opened before midnight and closed today are included.
