How this was built

  1. Each trade keeps its real fills, stop, exit and times. Profit and fees scale with the position, so the Scalper Offer's waived closing fee carries over.
  2. The record uses only shadow trades closed before the entry, so nothing from the future sets the leverage.
  3. A trade is liquidated when its recorded worst point crossed the liquidation price for its leverage and the market's maintenance margin. It then loses the $150 and the opening fee. Worst points come from 1-minute prices.
  4. A paper trade's shadow twin is the shadow trade of the same scanner, market, timeframe and side opened within 10 minutes of it.
  5. Trades that opened before midnight and closed today are included. The tracker's cells are left out.
