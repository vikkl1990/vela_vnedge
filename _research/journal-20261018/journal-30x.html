How this was built

  1. Each trade keeps its real fill prices, stop, exit and times. Profit and fees scale with the position size, so the Scalper Offer's waived closing fee carries over.
  2. A trade counts as liquidated when its recorded worst point crossed the liquidation price, which is 1 ÷ leverage minus the market's maintenance margin. A liquidation loses the full $100 margin plus the opening fee.
  3. The worst point comes from 1-minute prices, so a wick inside a minute can be missed. The liquidation counts are a floor.
  4. After the 30× balance reaches zero, its later trades are marked "account gone".
